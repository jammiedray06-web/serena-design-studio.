Skirt Pattern Lab

First experimental pattern module: a simple rectangular elastic-waist skirt. This is not yet a validated, production-ready sewing pattern.

Measure around the fullest part of the hips. Choose a finished skirt length from the waist to the desired hem. Use the same unit throughout. The engine adds configurable ease and seam/hem allowances.
Enter measurements and tap “Calculate pattern”.
Fit and print safety: sew a test version in inexpensive fabric, verify fit and construction, and check the 5 cm calibration square after printing. A correct square does not by itself validate garment fit. Print at 100% / actual size; do not use “fit to page.”

Project status

Working now: garment option controls, concept preview, on-device measurement saving, image upload, touch drawing, sketch PNG export, and experimental elastic-waist skirt calculations with a full-size SVG and calibration square.

Not ready yet: accurate cross-country numerical size charts, multi-piece bodice/trouser/jacket drafting, tiled PDF export, tested assembly instructions, cloud accounts, and Google Play packaging.
